It is not accepted as an implementation candidate at the measured byte result because Phase 3.5 requires total raw JS not to increase and still requires `3553` additional bytes of total-JS headroom.

- C1 lazy entire `TimelinePanel`: wrong ownership boundary because it defers the shell and non-chart states;

- C4 inert renderer: measurement-only upper bound, not product behavior.

No MP08 product implementation, branch, commit, PR, or merge is authorized at this point. No bundle budget increase or weakened total-JS gate is authorized.
