## Behavior preserved

- Ordered score/status tone precedence remains:
  1. healthy status or score at least 80;
  2. otherwise degraded status or score at least 50;
  3. otherwise unhealthy status or score below 50;
  4. otherwise neutral.
- A high score can therefore override degraded or unhealthy score color.
- Healthy status can retain healthy score color for a low score.
- Status badge wording remains independently derived by each caller from health status and availability.
- Null score remains `—`, neutral text/bar, and `0%` width.
- Numeric zero remains `0`, unhealthy text/bar, and `4%` width.
- Every non-null score below 4 retains the `4%` minimum width.
- Desktop continues to use the exact compact wrapper, layout, track, and bar classes.
- Mobile continues to use the exact regular wrapper, layout, track, and bar classes.
- Desktop row click, Enter/Space activation, `preventDefault`, role, tab index, accessible label, order, and identity are unchanged.
- Mobile native-button interaction, accessible label, order, identity, and responsive presentation are unchanged.
- No status-copy, metric-formatting, availability, routing, popup, query, API, or data-ownership behavior moved into the canonical component.
