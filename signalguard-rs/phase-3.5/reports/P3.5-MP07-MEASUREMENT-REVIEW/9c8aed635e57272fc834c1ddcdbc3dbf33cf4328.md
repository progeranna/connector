## Disposition

No MP07 route-splitting implementation is authorized.

R3 is accepted as the best measured initial-entry route prototype, and R2b is accepted as the better symbol-route ownership design if an initial-only tradeoff is ever reopened. Neither is an implementation candidate under current policy because every route split increases total raw JS.

No MP08 lazy-renderer implementation is authorized at its measured `+852` total raw result.

The route and lazy-chart experiments together prove that code splitting alone cannot satisfy the remaining `3553`-byte total-JS reduction requirement. Bundle policy must not be weakened and the budget must not be raised to accept these initial-only improvements.

## Next evidence required

The next authorized work should measure a true total-JS reduction at the chart implementation boundary rather than another chunk-placement experiment. The preferred experiment is a Recharts-free, behavior-compatible native SVG renderer that preserves the accepted Timeline shell, states, chart semantics, accessibility, responsive behavior, tooltip behavior, anomaly markers, and visual presentation.

Any such experiment remains read-only until exact build, behavior, visual, and byte evidence is reviewed.
